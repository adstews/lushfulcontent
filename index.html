Frequently Asked Questions

Is the procedure painful?
Most patients report little to no pain thanks to local anesthetic. Laughing gas is also provided for anxiety relief. Patients are often surprised at how comfortable the procedure is.
How long do results last?
Results last 2-3 years in the shaft and approximately 1 year in the glans. Most patients never fully return to their original baseline due to collagen formation. Touch-ups are typically scheduled every 12-18 months.
Does it affect erectile function or sensitivity?
No. GirthFill does not affect sensitivity or erection quality in any way. This is purely an aesthetic procedure.
Is it reversible?
Yes. Since we use hyaluronic acid filler, it can be dissolved completely if desired. The filler also naturally metabolizes over time.
How much girth can I expect to gain?
Patients can expect to increase girth by 0.25 to 2 inches depending on the amount of filler used. A 1-inch increase in girth equals approximately a 25% increase in thickness for the average person.
What is the recovery time?
Mild tenderness and swelling for a few days to two weeks. Work and light activity can resume in 1-2 days. No sexual activity for a minimum of 2 weeks.
Do I need to travel to NYC?
Lushful Aesthetics has locations in NYC and San Diego. Many patients travel from across the US and internationally. We provide a travel guide for out-of-town patients.
