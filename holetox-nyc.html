Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?
Most people describe it as a quick pinch. There are about four small injection sites, and we offer ProNox (laughing gas) to keep you relaxed and comfortable throughout.
How long does it last?
Results typically last 3–4 months. To maintain them and keep bottoming comfortable, most people return for a quick top-up about every three months.
Will it actually make bottoming easier?
Yes. By relaxing the internal anal sphincter, Holetox reduces the tightness and involuntary clenching that make receptive sex uncomfortable. Combined with regular dilation, it makes bottoming noticeably easier.
Are there side effects?
Side effects are minimal. We ask that you avoid working out for 24 hours and avoid anal sex or play for 3 days. Botox takes about two weeks to fully activate—give it the full two weeks before judging results.
Will it cause incontinence?
No. We dose only enough to relax the sphincter for comfort—not enough to affect its normal function.
Are there before & after photos?
No. Holetox is a performance-based treatment—it relaxes the muscle but doesn't create a visible change, so there's nothing to photograph.
Who isn't a candidate?
If you have an active anal or rectal condition—an active fissure, inflamed hemorrhoids, or similar—we'll ask you to see a board-certified proctologist first. We'll confirm that Holetox is right for you during your consult.
Do I need to come to NYC or San Diego?
Holetox is performed in person at our New York City and San Diego offices. The consult itself can be virtual.
